Terms
JFM
January–March. Snowfall is summed over these three months of each winter.
mm w.e.
Millimetres of water equivalent: the depth of liquid water if the snow melted. Fresh snow is often ~10× deeper, but the ratio varies.
Anomaly
Difference from the 1991–2020 average, after removing each location's long-term linear trend.
El Niño winter
One of the 14 moderate-to-strong El Niño winters in this dataset (the blog's 13 plus 2023–24).
RONI
Relative Oceanic Niño Index (NOAA CPC): tropical Pacific sea-surface temperature anomaly relative to the tropical mean, in °C. Shown for Dec–Feb; ≥ +1.0 is roughly a moderate El Niño, ≥ +1.5 strong, ≤ −0.5 La Niña.
